## Full description
(Field: "Full description" — max 4000 characters)

```
A study of the world's sovereign banners.

Learn Flags — Atlas Project is an offline, ad-free educational app that teaches you the national flags, geography, and flag history of all 197 sovereign nations of the world. Whether you're a quiz buff, a geography enthusiast, a teacher, or simply curious, the app gives you four ways to learn.

FOUR WAYS TO LEARN

• Flashcards. Flip a card to reveal each country's name, its native-language name, and a short paragraph on what the colors, shapes, and emblems of its flag actually mean. From Japan's Hinomaru to South Africa's post-apartheid Y-shape to the Pan-African and Pan-Arab color schemes, every flag has a story.

• Quick Quiz. A flag appears, and you choose from four country names. Build streaks, beat your best, focus on a single continent if you want to drill specific regions.

• World Map. A flag and country name appear — click the right country on a real world map with accurate borders. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, or jump straight to a continent.

• Cartographer. The hardest mode. A flag appears with the country name hidden. Place it on the map blind for 2 points, peek at the name first for 1 point, or miss for none. A pure test of what you know.

FEATURES

• All 197 sovereign nations
• Real country borders, not abstract shapes
• Native country names (e.g. Japan / 日本 / Nihon, Germany / Deutschland)
• Short factual paragraphs on each flag's design and symbolism
• Filter by continent: Africa, Americas, Asia, Europe, Oceania
• Works fully offline after first launch
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking, no in-app purchases
• Designed for phones, tablets, and Chromebooks

For learners of every age, from primary-school students to lifelong geography lovers.
```

## Data safety form
The Data Safety section is what Google uses to populate the "Data shared" /
"Data collected" labels on your store listing. Answer:

- **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?**
  → **No**

- **Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?**
  → **Not applicable** (no data is collected, so no data is in transit)

- **Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted?**
  → **Not applicable** (no data is collected)

The store listing will display the "No data collected · No data shared" badge,
which is genuinely accurate.
