Privacy Policy

Learn Flags — Atlas Project · Last updated: May 25, 2026

This is the privacy policy for the Android app and web app Learn Flags — Atlas Project.

The short version

This app does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information. It works entirely offline once installed. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

What data the app accesses

The app contains the following data, all of it bundled into the app or fetched once at install:

  • Country names, native-language names, and short factual descriptions of each national flag
  • Public-domain country border data
  • SVG images of national flags
  • Your in-app progress (e.g. which flashcards you have marked as learned) is stored locally on your device only, in standard browser local storage. It is never transmitted anywhere.

What the app does NOT do

  • The app does not create user accounts or require sign-in.
  • The app does not collect personal information of any kind — no name, email, phone, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.
  • The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • The app does not transmit your activity, progress, or scores to any server.
  • The app does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

Network use

The app uses the network only on first launch, to download the app's offline content. After that initial download, the app works without any internet connection. No data is sent from your device to any server at any time.

Children's privacy

This app is suitable for all ages and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because the app collects no information from any user, regardless of age, it is safe for use by children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please open an issue at https://github.com/jefrix/learnflags/issues.
